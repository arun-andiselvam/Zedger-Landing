We've tested the three most popular expense splitting apps available in India — **Splitwise**, **[Zedger App](/)**, and **Tricount** — to help you pick the right one for your needs.

## 1. Splitwise — The Popular Choice

Splitwise is probably the first app that comes to mind when you think of splitting expenses. It's been around since 2011 and has a massive user base globally.

### What's Good

- **Well-known**: Most of your friends probably already have it
- **Simple interface**: Easy to add expenses and see balances
- **Currency support**: Works with INR and other currencies
- **Settle up suggestions**: Shows the minimum number of transactions needed

### What's Not So Good

- **Free version is limited**: You can only add 5 expenses per day without Pro
- **No offline mode**: Need internet to add expenses (problematic during trips)
- **Recurring expenses locked**: Need Pro subscription (₹999/year) for auto-recurring bills
- **Gets expensive**: Pro pricing has increased significantly over the years
- **Not designed for Indian families**: The "friends splitting bills" model doesn't fit joint family expense tracking

### Best For

Groups of friends who occasionally split restaurant bills or trip expenses, and don't mind the daily limit.

## 2. Zedger — The Indian Alternative

[Zedger App](/) is a newer player built specifically for how Indians manage shared money. Unlike apps designed for Western "splitting bills" culture, Zedger understands that Indian families often share expenses differently.

### What's Good

- **Shareable ledger books**: Create separate books for family, roommates, trips — each with its own members
- **Built for Indian families**: Designed for joint family expenses, not just friend groups
- **Truly free**: No daily limits on expenses, core features available free
- **Offline mode**: Add expenses even without internet — syncs when you're back online
- **4 book types**: Family Book, Monthly Recurring, Friends Trip, and Money Collection
- **Smart splitting**: Split equally, by percentage, by shares, or exact amounts
- **Minimal ads**: Free version has occasional ads, not intrusive

### What's Not So Good

- **Newer app**: Smaller user base compared to Splitwise
- **No web version yet**: Mobile app only (Android & iOS)

### Best For

Indian families tracking household expenses, roommates sharing rent and utilities, and groups who need offline access during trips.

**[Download Zedger App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hitasoft.zedger)** — Available on Android and iOS.

## 3. Tricount — The European Option

Tricount is popular in Europe and has gained some users in India, especially among travelers.

### What's Good

- **No account required**: Can start using immediately without signup
- **Simple and clean**: Very straightforward interface
- **Good for one-time trips**: Easy to create, use, and forget
- **Offline capable**: Works without internet

### What's Not So Good

- **Basic features**: Lacks advanced splitting options
- **No recurring expenses**: Have to add every expense manually
- **Limited to trips**: Not ideal for ongoing household expense tracking
- **European-focused**: Currency conversion and features designed for Europe

### Best For

One-time trip expense tracking with a small group, especially if members don't want to create accounts.

## Feature Deep-Dive

### For Families

If you're tracking household expenses with family members, **[Zedger App](/)** is the clear winner. Its "Family Book" feature is designed exactly for this:

- Parents and adult children can all contribute
- Track groceries, utilities, medical expenses, and more
- See monthly summaries of who spent what
- No awkward "you owe me" dynamics — it's a shared family ledger

Splitwise and Tricount treat every expense as something to be "split and settled," which doesn't match how Indian families actually work.

### For Roommates

All three apps work for roommates, but here's the difference:

- **Splitwise**: Good if everyone already has it, but daily limits are frustrating
- **[Zedger App](/)**: Best if you want to track rent, utilities, groceries, and maid payments in one place with no limits
- **Tricount**: Works, but lacks recurring expense features

### For Travel

When you're on a trip with friends:

- **Tricount**: Quick setup, no accounts needed, works offline
- **[Zedger App](/)**: Friends Trip Book feature, offline mode, detailed splitting
- **Splitwise**: Works, but needs internet and has daily limits

## The Verdict

**Choose Splitwise if**: All your friends already use it and you only need to split occasional expenses (within the daily limit).

**Choose [Zedger App](/) if**: You want a truly free expense tracker for family, roommates, or trips — especially if you need offline mode or are tired of Splitwise's limitations.

**Choose Tricount if**: You need a quick, account-free solution for a one-time trip with friends.
